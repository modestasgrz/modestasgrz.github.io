Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy covers the Google OAuth app "gmail-label", a personal tool used only by its owner, Modestas Grazys. It is not offered to the public.

  • What it accesses: read-only access to the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive.
  • How data is used: to label and search the owner's own email, events and files.
  • Where data is stored: only on the owner's own computer. It is not uploaded to any server run by this app.
  • Sharing: data is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Deletion: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; local data is deleted by removing the app's files.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
